Diagnostic Pipeline

Standardized workflow from raw data ingestion to neural inference.

Phase 01

Data Ingestion

Secure upload of single-row CSV files containing 178 discrete EEG features extracted from continuous brain signal recordings.

Phase 02

Preprocessing

Automated Z-score standardization followed by Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to eliminate noise and isolate critical variance.

Phase 03

SVM Inference

Processed vectors are fed into our optimized Support Vector Machine to classify the presence of imminent or active seizure states.
